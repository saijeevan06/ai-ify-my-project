# 3-minute video — script + how to record it

## How the recording works

You talk out loud **while** you click through the live site. It's one take of screen plus voice together, like showing a friend your screen on a call. You don't type any narration anywhere.

**Pick a recorder**
- **Loom (easiest):** loom.com → free account → browser extension → “Screen only” (webcam bubble optional) → record. When you stop, it gives you a shareable link straight away. That link is your answer for “3-Minute Video”.
- **Windows Game Bar:** press `Win + G` → record. It saves an `.mp4`; upload it to Google Drive, set sharing to “Anyone with the link”, and paste that link.

**Before you hit record**
1. Open the live site in an **incognito window** with demo mode on: `LIVE_URL/?demo=1`
2. In the **Demo mode** panel (bottom right), click **Reset journey**, so nothing is registered yet.
3. Maximise the window (about 1440×900), zoom 100–110%, hide bookmarks.
4. Do one practice run without recording to learn where to click. Then record. The first real take is usually fine.

The admin key is `aiify-admin`.

## The take — what to click, what to say

Say it in your own words, using the lines below as a checklist. Don't read them word for word.

| Time | Click | Say (roughly) |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:20** | Nothing yet. You're on the landing page. | “The brief: 500 final-year students registered for a free AI workshop, in 7 days, with ₹2,000. First I did the maths. At student ad prices, ₹2,000 buys about 57 registrations, around 11% of the target. So this can't be an ad campaign. It has to be a product students share.” |
| **0:20–0:50** | Demo panel → **1. AI-ify “Smart parking system”**. Let it type and run. | “The insight: final-years in placement season don't want to *learn AI*. They want *their own project* to stand out. So the site gives value before any form. You type the project you already have…” (the steps run) “…it reads it, finds AI opportunities, and builds an upgrade.” |
| **0:50–1:15** | Scroll the blueprint, then click **Share card**. | “Smart parking became *AI Smart Parking Intelligence*, with four upgrades, a stack, difficulty, build time and a 9.1 score. It's designed to be screenshotted. This share card is a 1080×1350 image made for WhatsApp, with a pre-written message. That's share moment number one.” Close the modal. |
| **1:15–1:35** | **Reserve My Spot**. The form is prefilled in demo mode, so click **Reserve My Spot** again. | “Registration is six fields, under 30 seconds, and it comes *after* the value. Now I'm in: my own referral link, and a tracker for three friends, which unlocks the AI Builder Pack.” Click **Copy link** to show the toast. |
| **1:35–2:00** | Demo panel → **3. A friend joins**, three times. Then scroll to the leaderboard. | “Each friend who registers through my link moves the tracker. One, two, three: unlocked. And every registration counts for my college. My college is now #3 in the AI Builders Cup. That rivalry is share moment number three.” |
| **2:00–2:25** | Go to **/admin**, enter key `aiify-admin`, tick **Include demo users**. Then open **/qr**. | “This is what the growth team sees: visitors, blueprints, registrations against 500, the funnel, and every channel compared. Every poster, club and WhatsApp group gets its own tracked link and QR from this page.” |
| **2:25–2:45** | Go to **/deck**, scroll to slide 4, then slide 6. | “The plan: five channels. 200 from ambassadors in WhatsApp groups, 165 from referrals, posters, clubs, and ₹1,000 as one paid A/B test. And three experiments, starting with value-first versus form-first.” |
| **2:45–3:00** | Stay on the deck. | “With another 24 hours: a hosted database so referrals work across phones, a WhatsApp bot for confirmations and reminders, and running test one on real traffic. That's AI-ify My Project. Thanks for watching.” |

**After you stop:** in Loom, click **Copy link**. That's the form answer. If a take goes badly, delete it and redo; nobody sees failed takes.
